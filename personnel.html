Personnel

Principal Investigators

Joseph Crompton, MD PhD

Principal Investigator

Dr. Crompton is a cancer surgeon and immunologist at UCLA; his research is centered on de-escalating morbid and unnecessary operations for cancer patients. His clinical focus is on the multi-modality treatment of soft tissue sarcoma, melanoma and cutaneous malignancies.

Scott Eggener, MD

Principal Investigator

Dr. Eggener is a urologic oncologist who specializes in the care of patients with prostate, testicular, and kidney cancers. His research on urologic cancers, primarily on prostate and testicular cancer, has resulted in over 350 peer-reviewed publications.

Jessica Rett, PhD

Principal Investigator

Dr. Rett is a linguist at UCLA whose research focuses on the meaning of words and sentences, and how meanings change from context to context.
